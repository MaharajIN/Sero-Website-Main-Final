Corporate and gifting

Before you ask for a quote

How does a corporate order work?

Tell us how many gifts you need and the date. We send a quote, you pick the bars, the logo and the words, and we pack every box by hand and ship it free.

What does it cost?

Gift boxes start from $18 in the shop. Corporate orders ship free, and volume pricing starts from 25 gifts. We send a quote once you tell us how many you need.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 5 to 7 business days. If you need the gifts by a certain date, put it in your message.

Can I add my logo and a handwritten note?

Yes. Your logo can go on the bar sleeves and the note card, and every note is handwritten by us, one card per gift.
