Since2018

It started with one cup of hot chocolate on a farm porch.

In 2018, three of us drank hot chocolate on a farm in Izabal. It tasted nothing like the bars we knew, so we rented a workshop and promised to visit every farm we buy from.

Today more than 500 farming families grow cacao for Sero, in four regions of Guatemala. We still visit, and every batch is still tasted by hand.

  1. 2018The first harvest: 12 farming families and 400 hand-made bars
  2. 2019Our first single-origin bar, Finca Kampura
  3. 2021Our own stone grinder, so every step happens under one roof
  4. 2025500+ farming families, and still small and careful

Before you order

Where do your beans come from?

From farms and cooperatives in Guatemala: Escuintla on the Pacific side, and Izabal and the Polochic valley in the east. Each bar is named after the place its beans came from.

How much is shipping, and when will it arrive?

Shipping is free on orders over $50 and $6 below that. Standard delivery takes about 5 to 7 business days, and you see the estimate at checkout.

Which bars are vegan or gluten-free?

The four single-origin dark bars are made from cacao, cocoa butter and sugar and are labelled vegan and gluten-free. Milk bars contain milk and some recipes contain nuts. Every product page lists its ingredients.

Do you have chocolate without added sugar?

Yes. The 70% No Sugar bar is made only from cacao and cocoa butter. Our cacao nibs, cocoa powder and cocoa paste have no added sugar either.

Can I add a gift note to my order?

Yes. In your cart, tick "This is a gift" and write your message. It goes on a card inside the parcel.

Do you make gifts for teams and clients?

Yes. Corporate orders ship free, volume pricing starts at 25 gifts, and we add your logo or a handwritten note. Ask for a quote from the contact page.

Do you sell to cafes, hotels and shops?

Yes. Our wholesale range has counter boxes of 10 and 40 bars and couverture for baking. You can order it from the shop or contact us for larger amounts.

What is Sero Club?

Our membership. Members get 10% off one order every month, early access to new harvests and a treat in their birthday month. Join from your account page.

How should I store chocolate?

Somewhere cool, dry and dark, away from strong smells. A cupboard is better than the fridge.
