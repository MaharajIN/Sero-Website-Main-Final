How to taste chocolate

  1. Look and snap

    A well-tempered bar shines and breaks cleanly.

  2. Smell

    Breathe in first. You may find fruit, nuts or caramel.

  3. Melt

    Let a square melt slowly on your tongue.

  4. Notice the finish

    See what stays after it is gone. Then try the next farm.

Questions, answered plainly

What does "bean to bar" mean?

It means we make the chocolate ourselves, starting from the raw cacao bean: sorting, roasting, cracking, grinding, refining and tempering. Most chocolate brands buy ready-made chocolate and reshape it. We start one step earlier.

Where do your beans come from?

From farms and cooperatives in Guatemala: Escuintla on the Pacific side, and Izabal and the Polochic valley in the east. Each bar is named after the place its beans came from.

Can I visit the farms or the kitchen?

We are planning visits. Use the contact page to tell us you are interested and we will write back when dates are set.

Do you ship abroad, and what about gifts for teams?

Yes. Corporate orders ship free, and we make custom gift boxes with your logo or a handwritten note. See gifting or send a request on the contact page.

Are your bars vegan or allergen-free?

Our dark bars are made from cacao and a little sugar, and several are vegan. Milk bars contain milk, and some recipes contain nuts. Every product page lists its ingredients and diet notes.

How should I store chocolate?

Keep it cool, dry and away from sunlight and strong smells. A pantry or cupboard is best. The fridge can pick up smells and dull the flavour, and a sunny window melts it.
