Gifting, answered

Good to know before you give

How does gifting work?

Tell us how many gifts you need and the date. We send a quote, you pick the bars, the logo and the words, and we pack every box by hand and ship it free.

Who can send a gift, and to whom?

Anyone. Companies gift to clients, teams and partners, and individuals can send our gift boxes to friends and family. Tell us who it is for and we will help you choose.

What occasions do you cover?

Birthdays, Christmas, thank-you gifts, Valentine's, membership boxes, the office pantry and signature gifts. Not sure which fits? Ask and we will suggest one.

What does it cost?

Gift boxes start from $18 in the shop. Corporate orders ship free, and volume pricing starts from 25 gifts. Once you tell us how many you need, we send you a quote.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 5 to 7 business days. If you need the gifts by a certain date, tell us when you ask for the quote.

Can I add my logo and a handwritten note?

Yes. Your logo can go on the bar sleeves and the note card. Every note is handwritten by us, one card per gift: one message for everyone, or a different one for each person.

What if a gift arrives damaged?

Report it within 7 days and we replace it or refund you. Bars travel packed in straw with cold packs, so they arrive the way they left our kitchen.
