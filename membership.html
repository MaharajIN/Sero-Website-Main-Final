Good to know

Clear terms, no surprises

Does membership cost anything?

Membership details and any fee are shown in full when you join from your account page, before you confirm.

Does the 10% apply to every order?

It applies to one order each month, on any products in your cart. It renews on the first of the month.

How do I buy a members-only product?

Add it to your cart like any other product. It sits in the same order and ships together.

Can I leave at any time?

Yes. You can switch off membership from your account whenever you like.

Does membership help the farmers?

Yes. Every bar is made from cacao we buy from four named farms in Guatemala, so each member order supports the people who grow it.
